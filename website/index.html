Integrations

Sell in store and online — one stock

Connect your Shopify store to KDSPOZ in one tap. Products, stock, and online orders stay in sync automatically, so you never oversell.

  • Import products

    Bring your Shopify catalogue, variants, SKUs, and barcodes into the POS.

  • Live stock sync

    Every counter sale, purchase, or stock change updates your online store.
